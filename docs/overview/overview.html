2How it all hangs together

There are five services working together behind the scenes. Here's what each one does, no jargon.

Service What it does
GitHub Where the code lives. Think Dropbox for code, except it keeps every version you've ever saved, so you can always go back.
Netlify Your web host. Every time GitHub changes, Netlify rebuilds the site and puts it live (dreamy-taffy-27a291.netlify.app). It also runs the little "server function" that keeps your Anthropic key out of sight.
Anthropic The company behind Claude. Every agent answer comes from them, charged per use to your Anthropic account.
Supabase Your database. It's set up and ready to go, and will hold agent memory, chat history and the activity log once there's a proper login.
Claude Code Claude as your developer, on your own computer. You tell it what you want; it makes the change, checks it works, and saves it.

What's not there yet (totally normal at this stage!)

The agents can't search the web yet

Heads up on this one: if you ask HUNTER for "live tenders", it'll write convincing ones, but they're made up. Until we add web search, treat research as ideas to check, not gospel.

They forget things

Chats disappear when you refresh, and the agents don't remember past chats or your clients. Memory's the next big step.

"24/7" is the goal, not yet the reality

Right now the agents only work while you're chatting to them. Morning reports, auto follow-ups and tracking opens are on the list.

Long answers get cut short

Answers are capped to fit the host's time limit. "Streaming" (words appearing as they're written) fixes that and feels much snappier too.

4How you can keep building it

You don't need to code at all. You tell Claude what you want, have a look, and decide if it goes live. That's it.

How it works

  1. Just ask. "Add an agent that writes quotes." "COMPOSER's emails are too long."
  2. Claude tells you the plan in a few lines. You say yes (or tweak it).
  3. Claude makes the change, checks nothing's broken, and makes a save point.
  4. You have a look on your own computer first. Claude tells you what to click.
  5. "Put it live" and it's on the website a couple of minutes later.
    "Undo that" and it's back how it was.

Safety rails (always on)

🔑 No keys in the code. Claude physically can't write a secret key into a file.

✅ Can't finish with it broken. Claude can't say "done" until the site builds and the work's saved.

🙋 Nothing goes live without you. Claude always checks with you first.

♻️ Nothing gets lost. Deleting work is blocked, and every version is kept.

Who does what

You, with Claude Give me a shout
New agents, tweaking how agents write, quick tasks, wording, colours, layout, and the "small" ideas on the next page Logins, the database, anything with money or emails going out, hooking up other systems (CRM, Gmail), or any time Claude says "this is a bigger change"

A few good habits: one change at a time · always have a look before "put it live" · never paste a key into the chat · if in doubt, make a save point first.
